# Tips, Tip Pooling and Payroll Export

## Adding tips after payment
Servers can adjust a tip on a card payment until the batch closes, which happens automatically at 4:00 AM. After the batch closes the tip can no longer be changed from the POS.

## Setting up a tip pool
In Settings, Tips, create a pool and assign roles with a point weight. For example, servers at 10 points, bussers at 4 points and bartenders at 6 points share the pooled tips in proportion to points multiplied by hours worked.

## Exporting payroll hours and tips
Open Reports, Payroll and choose a pay period, then press Export. SousChef supports CSV and direct exports to Gusto and ADP; hours and tips are listed per employee.

## Tip-out for support staff
A Tip-Out Rule takes a percentage of a server's tips and credits it to a role. The default is 3 percent of net sales to the bar and 2 percent to the host, and rules can be edited by owners only.
