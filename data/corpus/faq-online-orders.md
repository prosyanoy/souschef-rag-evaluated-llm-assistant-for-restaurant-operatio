# Online Ordering and Delivery FAQ

## Setting up online ordering
Turn on Online Ordering under Channels and SousChef creates a free ordering page for your restaurant. Online menu items sync from the POS, and online orders print on the kitchen printer automatically.

## Online ordering fees
There is no commission on orders placed through your own ordering page; only the standard card processing rate applies. Orders accepted through third-party marketplaces are charged the marketplace's own commission.

## Pickup and prep times
Set a default prep time between 10 and 60 minutes under Channels, Prep Time. Guests see the estimated pickup time at checkout, and you can add 15 minutes with one tap when the kitchen is busy.

## Delivery radius
Restaurants can deliver with their own drivers within a radius of up to 8 miles. Set the radius and the delivery fee in Channels, Delivery, and orders outside the radius are rejected at checkout.
