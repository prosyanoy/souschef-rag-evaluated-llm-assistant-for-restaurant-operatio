# Offline Mode and Sync

## What works when the internet is down
Offline Mode keeps taking orders, sending tickets to local printers and accepting cash for up to 72 hours. Online-only features such as gift card lookups, online orders and cloud reports are paused until the connection returns.

## Taking card payments offline
Card payments taken offline are stored encrypted on the terminal and submitted automatically when the connection returns. The offline limit is $100 per transaction, and the restaurant carries the risk if a stored card is later declined.

## How syncing works after reconnecting
When the terminal reconnects it uploads queued orders in the order they were created and then downloads menu changes. A Sync Complete banner appears at the top of the screen; large backlogs can take up to 10 minutes.
