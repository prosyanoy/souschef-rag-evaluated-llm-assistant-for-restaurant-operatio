# Billing and Subscription FAQ

## Subscription plans and prices
SousChef POS offers three plans: Starter at $69 per month, Pro at $149 per month and Enterprise with custom pricing for groups of 5 or more locations. Each plan includes unlimited menu items and staff accounts.

## Free trial
Every new account gets a 14 day free trial of the Pro plan without entering a credit card. At the end of the trial the account drops to read-only mode until you pick a plan.

## Cancelling a subscription
Owners can cancel any time under Account, Billing, Cancel Plan. Service continues until the end of the paid month, and exported data stays downloadable for 90 days.

## Hardware purchases and warranty
Terminals, printers and card readers carry a 12 month warranty. Defective hardware is replaced with a pre-paid return label, and shipping takes 2 to 4 business days.
