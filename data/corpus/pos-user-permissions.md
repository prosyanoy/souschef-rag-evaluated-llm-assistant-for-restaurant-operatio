# Staff Roles, PINs and Permissions

## Adding an employee
Go to Staff, press Add Employee, and enter a name, role and four-digit PIN. The employee can clock in on any terminal right after saving.

## Default roles and permissions
SousChef ships with four roles: Server, Bartender, Manager and Owner. Only Managers and Owners can approve voids after firing, open the cash drawer without a sale, and change prices.

## Resetting a forgotten PIN
A manager can reset any employee PIN from Staff by pressing Reset PIN, which sets a temporary code that must be changed at the next login. Managers cannot reset the Owner PIN; the owner must use the email recovery link.

## Locking out former employees
Set an employee to Inactive in Staff to block logins immediately while keeping their sales history and tip records. Inactive employees can be reactivated at any time.
