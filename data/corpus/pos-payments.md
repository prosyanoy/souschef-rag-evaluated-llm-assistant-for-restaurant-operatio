# Card Payments, Fees and Chargebacks

## Processing fees
SousChef Payments charges 2.6 percent plus 10 cents per in-person card transaction and 2.9 percent plus 30 cents for keyed-in or online payments. There are no monthly minimums or PCI fees.

## Payout schedule
Card sales are deposited to your bank account the next business day when the batch closes before 4:00 AM. Weekend sales arrive on Monday, and Instant Payout is available for a 1 percent fee.

## Responding to a chargeback
When a guest disputes a charge you receive an email and a Disputes notice in the dashboard. Upload the signed receipt and the itemized check within 7 days, otherwise the dispute is lost automatically.

## Tap to pay and chip readers
The Countertop Reader accepts chip, tap and mobile wallets. If the reader says Card Read Error, ask the guest to insert the chip instead of tapping, or clean the contacts with a dry cloth.
