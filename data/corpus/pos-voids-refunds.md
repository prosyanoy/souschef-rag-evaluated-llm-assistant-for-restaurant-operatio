# Voids, Comps and Refunds

## Voiding an item before it is sent
Tap the item on the open check and choose Remove. Items that have not been sent to the kitchen disappear without a trace and need no manager approval.

## Voiding an item after it is sent
Once an item has been fired, a void requires a manager PIN and a reason code such as Wrong Item or Guest Changed Mind. The void is printed on the kitchen printer as a cancellation ticket and recorded in the Void Report.

## Comping items
A comp discounts an item by 100 percent while keeping it in sales reporting as a comp. Servers can comp up to $15 per check; anything higher needs a manager PIN.

## Refunding a closed check
Closed checks can be refunded from Check History within 30 days. Card refunds return to the original card in 3 to 5 business days, while cash refunds open the cash drawer.
