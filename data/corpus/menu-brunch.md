# Maison Basil Weekend Brunch Menu

## Brunch hours and bottomless drinks
Brunch is served Saturday and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 2:30 PM. Bottomless mimosas are $22 per person with a 90 minute limit and require a food order.

## Brunch plates
- Eggs Benedict ($17): poached eggs, Canadian bacon and hollandaise on an English muffin. Contains: egg, dairy, gluten.
- Buttermilk Pancakes ($14): three pancakes with maple butter and berries. Contains: dairy, egg, gluten.
- Shakshuka ($16): eggs baked in spiced tomato sauce with feta and grilled bread. Vegetarian.

## Kids brunch
Children under 10 can order the Kids Brunch for $9, which includes one pancake or scrambled eggs, fruit and a small juice. Refills of juice are free.
