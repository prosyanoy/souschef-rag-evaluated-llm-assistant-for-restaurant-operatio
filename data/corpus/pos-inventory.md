# Inventory and Par Levels

## Setting par levels
A par level is the quantity you want on the shelf after a delivery. Enter it on the ingredient page, and SousChef flags the ingredient on the Low Stock list when counts drop below 25 percent of par.

## Recipe costing
Link ingredients to a menu item through its recipe card. Food cost percentage is calculated as ingredient cost divided by menu price and updates whenever you enter a new supplier invoice price.

## Counting stock
Start a Stock Count from Inventory, Counts and walk through the storage areas in order. Variances above 5 percent are highlighted in red so you can investigate waste or theft.

## Ordering from suppliers
Press Create Purchase Order on the Low Stock list to build an order from your par levels. Orders can be emailed or sent as a PDF, and received quantities update stock when you mark the delivery as received.
