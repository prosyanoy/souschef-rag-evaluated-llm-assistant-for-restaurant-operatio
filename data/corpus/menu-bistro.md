# Maison Basil Bistro Dinner Menu

## Starters
- French Onion Soup ($9): caramelized onions, beef broth and melted Gruyere. Contains: dairy, gluten.
- Burrata Salad ($14): burrata, heirloom tomatoes and basil oil. Contains: dairy.
- Crispy Calamari ($13): lightly fried squid with lemon aioli. Contains: gluten, egg, shellfish.

## Mains
- Steak Frites ($32): 10 oz hanger steak, shallot butter and fries. Contains: dairy.
- Mushroom Risotto ($24): arborio rice, wild mushrooms and parmesan. Vegetarian. Contains: dairy.
- Pan-Seared Salmon ($29): crispy skin salmon over lentils with dill cream. Contains: fish, dairy.

## Desserts
- Crème Brûlée ($10): vanilla custard with a caramelized sugar crust. Contains: dairy, egg. Gluten free.
- Chocolate Fondant ($11): warm chocolate cake with a molten centre. Contains: dairy, egg, gluten.
- Lemon Sorbet ($7): house-made sorbet. Vegan and gluten free.

## Dietary and allergen policy
The kitchen handles wheat, dairy, nuts, egg, fish and shellfish, so cross-contact is possible. Gluten-free pasta can be substituted in any pasta dish for a $3 upcharge. Please tell your server about allergies before ordering.
