# Reports and End-of-Day Close

## Running the end-of-day close
Press End of Day in the Manager menu after all checks are closed. The close prints the Z report, locks the day's sales and automatically closes the card batch.

## Sales by category report
Reports, Sales, Category Mix shows revenue and item counts per menu category. Use the date range picker to compare weeks, and press Export to download the data as a CSV file.

## Finding why the till is off
Open Reports, Cash Drawer to compare expected cash with the counted amount. The report lists every no-sale opening, paid-in and paid-out, so you can trace a shortage back to a single transaction.

## Scheduled report emails
Under Reports, Schedules you can email the daily sales summary to up to 5 recipients at a time you choose. Owners receive it at 6:00 AM by default.
