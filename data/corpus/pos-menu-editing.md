# Editing Menu Items, Modifiers and 86ing

## Adding a new menu item
Open Menu Setup, choose a category and press Add Item. Enter a name, price and tax group; new items appear on every terminal within 60 seconds.

## Creating modifiers
Modifiers such as Extra Cheese or No Onion are grouped in a modifier group. Set a group to Required when the server must pick an option, and add an upcharge to any option that costs extra.

## 86ing an item
When the kitchen runs out of something, long-press the item and choose 86. The item is greyed out on all terminals and online ordering immediately, and it returns automatically at the next day's opening unless you un-86 it earlier.

## Scheduling happy hour pricing
Create a Price Schedule under Menu Setup and select the days, start time and end time. Items listed in the schedule switch to their happy hour price automatically and switch back at the end time.
