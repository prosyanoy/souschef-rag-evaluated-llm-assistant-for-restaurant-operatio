# Kitchen Printer and KDS Troubleshooting

## Kitchen printer is not printing tickets
First check that the printer shows a green status light and that the paper roll is loaded. Then open Settings, Hardware, Printers and press Test Print; if the test fails, power-cycle the printer and confirm it is on the same network as the terminal.

## Printing to the wrong station
Each menu item is routed to a print station by its Category Routing rule. If drinks print in the kitchen, open the item's category in Menu Setup and change its Routing to Bar Printer.

## Kitchen display screens freezing or lagging
A kitchen display (KDS) that freezes usually has a weak Wi-Fi signal. Move the access point closer or connect the display with an Ethernet cable, then restart the KDS app from the screen's menu.

## Receipt printer prints blank paper
Blank receipts mean the thermal paper is loaded upside down. Open the lid, flip the roll so the paper unwinds from the bottom, and close the lid until it clicks.
